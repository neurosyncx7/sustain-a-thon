# Data attribution

- `stars.bin`: stars to magnitude 7.2 (RA, Dec, V mag, B-V as float32), converted from
  d3-celestial `data/stars.8.json` (Hipparcos-derived). Copyright (c) 2015 Olaf Frohn, BSD-3-Clause.
- `india-outline.json`: Natural Earth 10m admin-0 countries, India point-of-view edition
  (`ne_10m_admin_0_countries_ind`), public domain, simplified to 0.02 deg.
